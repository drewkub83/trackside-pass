## Support series (Oct 23 2026)
Support series are `CHAMPS` entries in `data/champs.js` with `optIn:true` (off until turned on), `viaSupport:true` (their races are found in an event's `support` list, not `s`) and `parent:"<main series id>"`. Settings > My races and series shows the main series (draggable) and below them a "Support series" section, one folding group per parent ("With IMSA", "With Formula 1", ...; `group` on the parent overrides its name there), plus "Other series" for opt-in series without a parent (HSR); `renderFollowSub()` in `js/app.js`. To add one: a CHAMPS entry with `parent`, and its name in the `support` list of each event it races at, from the series' published calendar only (never guessed: if a calendar is not out, add the entry and say so in its `note`). Not yet in: Indy NXT and USF Pro (2027 schedules not published as of Oct 23 2026), and the round-by-round 2027 SRO America line-ups (GT America, GT4 America, TC America).

## Home screen: a bar with five tabs (Oct 2026)
The home screen has a floating bar (`#homeTabs`, `syncHomeTabs()` / `homeTab()` in `js/app.js`): **Home** (`#home`: Next up tile, Your series next up, Passport), **Series** (`#seriesList`: the series list, grouped into categories once you follow 5 or more series across 2 or more categories: `CATS` and `cat` on each series in `data/champs.js` (Open-wheel, Endurance, GT, One-make and touring, Historic), text tiles like a series' (OPEN, ENDU, GT, ONE, HIST: no icons until the series logos are used), `renderChampList()` / `openSerCat()` in `js/app.js`, swipe-back and the back row return to the category list; with fewer series it stays one plain list under region headings; `tools/validate.js` fails a series without a valid `cat`; Settings > My races and series is not grouped by category yet; an "All tracks by country" table was tried and removed because a long list looked unattractive, so a return needs a cleaner design), **Podium** (`#play`, `renderPlay()`: season total, open games, scored weekends, How points work; red dot when a game is open), **Leaders** (`#board`, leaderboard) and **Account** (`#account`, `js/account.js`; shows your initial and a red dot for friend requests). Anything with its own tab is not repeated on Home. Settings stay behind the top-right button of each page. Leaders and Account only appear when `SB` in `js/account.js` is set. The friend-notification switch is in Settings too (`#setPush`, `acctSettingsRender()`). Swipe-back or the Android back button from a tab returns to Home; a series or track opened from Series goes back to Series (`seriesBack`, `openTrack(id,"seriesList")`). `html{touch-action:manipulation}` stops double-tap zoom. `gameLock()`/`gameTiming()` read the game's own track (not `cur`) so the Podium tab can list every game.

## Wide screens: two panes (unfolded foldable, tablet; Oct 2026)
`<html class="wide">` is set from `(min-width:700px) and (min-height:520px)` (an inline script in index.html before first paint, then `WIDE_MQ` / `onWideChange()` in `js/app.js`; a phone, even held sideways, never matches, so nothing else changes there). Then `showScreen()` becomes `showScreenWide()`: the left pane (`.screen.on`) is the page you are on, the right pane (`.screen.on2`) is what you open from it. Home keeps **Pick your podium** on the right until you open something; the other tabs show `#paneHint`; `series`, `passport`, `friendPass`, `recap` and `settings` (`WIDE_RIGHT_ONLY`) always open on the right. A track (`#detail`) spans both: the Race Day hub (`#hubView`, always visible) on the left and `data-wr` = `events` (the day's schedule, the default), `map` or `info` on the right (`setWideRight()`, `wideTab()`; the Hub tab is hidden). The left pane is `--leftw` = `clamp(340px,50vw,460px)` so on a foldable the split lands on the fold. Folding keeps your place (the right view becomes the tab you are on; a right-pane page becomes the screen) and unfolding fills the right pane. The layout CSS is the `html.wide` block at the bottom of `css/styles.css`. Not built: tabletop (half-folded) posture, hinge-aware spacing (needs the Device Posture API or native Android), a drag-to-resize split. Test by loading the page at 840x700 (the browser pane's emulated size does not fire resize events, so reload after resizing or call `onWideChange()`).

## Styles (Settings > Look and units > Style)
`<html data-style="race">` switches on the Race Control look: one clearly marked override block at the bottom of `css/styles.css` (tokens for light, auto-dark and
forced-dark, then component rules). Classic is the default and has no attribute. Preference key `paddock:style`; index.html applies it before first
paint; `js/app.js` has `stylePref/applyStyle/setStyle`. Fonts (Barlow, Barlow Condensed) are in the index.html link AND `FONT_CSS` in sw.js: keep
the two strings identical or the fonts are not saved for offline. Series stripe colours are the `--s-*` tokens, keyed by `data-s` on home rows.


## Third style: Ticket Stubs (Nov 4 2026)
Settings > Look and units > Style has Classic, Race Control and **Ticket Stubs** (`data-style="ticket"`, key `paddock:style` = `ticket`; a stored `program` value from the short-lived Race Program style, which the owner dropped after trying it for a day, is read as `ticket`). Race weekends are paper tickets: warm paper (`#F3EDE3`, dark `#1A1612`), ink outlines, and on the Next-up tile and the "This week" series cards a coloured **date stub** (one colour per series: F1 red, NASCAR yellow, WEC blue, MotoGP orange, IndyCar green, IMSA teal, GT America purple, GT Europe magenta, HSR brown; `--tkc` set per `data-s`) cut off by a dashed tear line with two notches (the `::before`/`::after` half-circles). `renderSeason()` emits a `.scStub` (month and day, hidden in the other styles) and `renderHero()` puts the series id in `data-s`. Type is Barlow Condensed (headings, stubs, labels) and Barlow: nothing new to download. The tab bar is a dark pill with a sun-yellow active pill, the Leaderboard has one ticket per player with a large rank, and small print is larger (14.5 px). The CSS block is at the bottom of `css/styles.css` ("STYLE: TICKET STUBS") with light, auto-dark and forced-dark tokens. Race Program (cobalt header, bunting, Archivo Black and Yellowtail) was removed; it is in git history before the commit "Ticket Stubs" if it is ever wanted again.

## Fourth style: Editorial (Nov 4 2026)
Settings > Look and units > Style also has **Editorial** (`data-style="editorial"`, `paddock:style` = `editorial`): off-white paper (`#FAFAF7`, dark `#121212`), black type in Outfit, hairline rules instead of boxes (cards, hub cards and rows are just a bottom border), one red accent (`#D62828`), a 112 px countdown numeral on the Next-up tile, a plain tab bar with the current page underlined in red, ink-block "live now" cards, and the same larger small print as Ticket Stubs. Chosen by the owner from a mock-up ("A · Editorial"). The CSS block is the last one in `css/styles.css` ("STYLE: EDITORIAL"). No new fonts.

## First-run walkthrough (Nov 4 2026)
`js/onboard.js` (loaded last, listed in `sw.js` SHELL): a full-screen tour for a brand-new install. Six steps: Welcome, What it does, Which series do you follow (the real `toggleFollow` switches for the main series), Pick a look (the real `setStyle`, previews live), At the track (the real `toggleAuto` and `toggleAlerts`, which ask for location permission only when switched on) and, only when `SB` is set, An account is optional (opens `openAccount()`). It opens once: an inline script in index.html sets `window.__fresh` when localStorage has no `paddock:` key at load, and the tour is skipped if `K.onboarded` is set; Skip, the last button and the account button all set it. Replay: Settings > About > "Replay the walkthrough" (`obOpen()`). Built from the app's tokens (`#obv` rules at the end of css/styles.css), so it follows every style and theme. Existing users never see it automatically (they already have `paddock:` keys). The native apps get it too (they load the site); a reinstall counts as fresh.
