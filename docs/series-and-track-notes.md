## Naval Base Coronado (NASCAR San Diego) built (Nov 3 2026)
The circuit is on aircraft pavement (streets, aprons, taxiways) that OpenStreetMap does not map as roads, so the lap is TRACED from NASCAR's course map (`NASCAR-Naval-Base-Coronado-course-map.jpg.webp`, an aerial image with the yellow circuit and turn badges 1-16) and placed on the ground with three points the owner marked on Google Maps: Turn 12 32.7114018,-117.2088137, Turn 5 32.7114540,-117.1894817 and the start/finish 32.7039323,-117.1964725. Turn 12 and Turn 5 fix scale (2.67 m per image pixel) and rotation (3.4 degrees); the start/finish check landed within about 35 m. Result: 3.45 mi against NASCAR's official 3.4 mi, 16 turns, and the overlay on Esri World Imagery tiles (zoom 17, fetched with curl as a reference only, never stored in the app) follows the pavement. The yellow outline was extracted by colour (hue 18-34) with gaps, so the waypoints in `tools/street-circuits/naval-base-coronado-lap.json` were hand-placed along it and snapped to the mask: accurate to about 25 m. Turn labels are the numbered badges snapped to the nearest lap point (the badges sit 50-117 m beside the line). `tools/street-circuits/permanent/naval-base-coronado.rb` builds it (`circuit_file`, `pit_file`; run the builder directly, NOT `new_track.py`, whose `make_config.py` step would overwrite the .rb). The pit lane is NOT published: `naval-base-coronado-pit.json` is a stand-in along the start/finish straight. The OpenStreetMap lots and restroom were removed (unofficial). Not done: gates, parking, restrooms, food, hangar club etc. from the 2026 facility map (`SD-26-1723704-San-Diego-Facility-Map-VS21-scaled.jpg` on nascarsandiego.com: oblique drawing, so it needs a projective fit using its T9-T15 markers and circuit outline), crossings (the pedestrian bridges on that map), session times and results for the 2027 race, lap record, elevation (flat). The race was run in June 2026 on this course; the 2027 date in the app is Jul 30 - Aug 1.

**Refinement (Nov 3 2026, later): OpenStreetMap already maps part of this course.** `highway=raceway` ways tagged `wikidata=Q135664661` (Coronado Street Course) exist for Runway Road, the chicanes and the start/finish straight (5 pieces, 2.4 km; shown pink on the standard map, which is what the owner pointed out). They are ground truth, so the traced lap was fitted to them with a similarity ICP (median 9 m after the fit; scale 0.954, rotation 4.9 degrees, shift 120 m east against the first guess from the owner's pins: the pins were corner labels, not exact corners), then points within 35 m of a raceway piece were snapped onto it and points within 12 m of a mapped street were snapped onto that street. The refined lap is 3.32 mi (official 3.4). The fifth piece (way 1530494784, 430 m along the avenue on the SE side, about 150 m from the lap) was left out of the fit: it may belong to another layout or be mis-mapped. To redo: query `way["highway"="raceway"](32.68,-117.24,32.74,-117.17)` from Overpass; the fitting scripts were scratchpad scripts (`refine.py`: ICP + snap). The standard OpenStreetMap map the builder shows draws these raceway pieces in pink.

**Owner's hand edit (Nov 3 2026, later still):** the owner imported the refined lap into the Track Builder, adjusted it against the streets and the pink OpenStreetMap raceway pieces, and downloaded `~/Downloads/naval-base-coronado.spec.json`. That file's `circuit.path` (226 points, 3.45 mi against NASCAR's 3.4), `turns` and `pit` replaced the lap, the turn labels and the pit stand-in (it stays within about 15 m median / 60 m worst of the automatic refinement). It is the copy in `tools/track-builder/naval-base-coronado.spec.json`. To rebuild after any change: `ruby tools/street-circuits/build_permanent.rb tools/street-circuits/permanent/naval-base-coronado.rb --replace`, then `python3 tools/track-data/split_tracks.py naval-base-coronado`, clear the OpenStreetMap badges (`T["pois"]=[]` through `build_route.load/save`) and rebuild the grid with `build_route.build(..., rle=True)` and `save` (the builder does not write the grid).
