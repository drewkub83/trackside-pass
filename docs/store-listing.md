## Full description (max 4000)
Trackside Pass is a race-weekend companion for fans who go to the track. Open it at the circuit and see what is on next, find your way around, and keep playing between races.

RACE DAY HUB
One page for the whole weekend: the next session on track, the weather and whether rain is on the way, results as they are published, and quick buttons to the nearest restroom or food stand.

MAPS AND WALKING DIRECTIONS
Maps of more than 90 tracks with gates, grandstands, parking, restrooms, food, first aid and best viewing spots. Ask for directions and the route follows real paths and crosses the circuit only at bridges and tunnels, with a walking time you can read at a glance.

WORKS WITH POOR SIGNAL
The app and every track map are saved on your phone, so it keeps working when the network does not.

SCHEDULES YOU CAN TRUST
Practice, qualifying and races for each weekend, in track time or your own time. Star the sessions you care about and get an alert before each one.

PICK YOUR PODIUM
A free prediction game: call the top three before each race and score points against the official result, with more for long shots and for picking early. Add friends and compete on a leaderboard.

PASSPORT
Collect a stamp for every track you visit and badges for what you have seen.

SERIES
Covers racing series including IMSA, IndyCar, Formula 1, MotoGP, NASCAR and the FIA World Endurance Championship, with more added each season.

EARLY BETA
This is an early test version and your feedback shapes it. In the app, open Settings and tap Send feedback to tell us what is confusing, broken or missing.

PRIVACY
No account is needed to use the app. An account only adds friends, leaderboards and syncing. Anonymous usage counts help us improve the app and can be switched off in Settings.

Trackside Pass is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any racing series, team or track. Series and track names belong to their owners.
