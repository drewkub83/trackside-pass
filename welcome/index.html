Your race weekend, in your pocket.

Track maps, walking directions, schedules, weather and a live Race Day hub for the tracks you go to. Built for race day, so it keeps working with one bar of signal.

One page for the whole weekend

Open any track and the Race Day hub shows what is on next, the weather and whether rain is coming, and the nearest restroom or food stand. Star the sessions you care about and get an alert before each one.

  • Practice, qualifying and race times in track time or your own
  • Live timing links and results as they are published
  • What to watch: the drivers and cars worth following

Find your way around the track

Maps of more than 90 tracks, with gates, grandstands, parking, restrooms, food and first aid. Ask for directions and the walking route follows real paths and crosses the circuit only at bridges and tunnels.

  • Walking time to anything on the map
  • Notes on steep climbs and drops where the ground has hills
  • The app and every track's map are saved on your phone for poor signal

Pick your podium, even from the couch

Before each race, call the top three. Picks are scored against the official result, with more points for long shots and for picking early. Play against friends and keep a season score, so there is a reason to open the app between races.

  • A leaderboard with your friends
  • A Passport that stamps the tracks you visit
  • A recap after every race weekend

Add it to your phone in a minute

It is a web app, so there is nothing to download from a store. Open the app first, then add it to your home screen so it opens like any other app.

iPhone
  1. Tap Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Want race reminders, rain alerts and the lock screen live card? Those need the iPhone app: join the beta on TestFlight (you will be asked to install Apple's free TestFlight app first).

Android
  1. Tap Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Open the menu (the three dots).
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Want push alerts and the full app? Join the Android beta on Google Play. It is invite-only while it is tested, so if the link says you cannot join, ask for access with your Gmail address and we will add you.

Help me test it

I am building this on my own and I want real fans to try it at real events. If something is confusing, broken or missing, tell me: open Settings in the app and tap Send feedback. Every note gets read.
