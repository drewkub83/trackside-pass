## What this means for the app
- Series-level data with an "as of" date and a source link is accurate and cheap to keep. Per-weekend channels (which NASCAR race is on which network, which IMSA session is Peacock Premium) change weekly: link to the series' TV page instead of copying it.
- Do NOT show the 2026 rights as 2027 facts. MotoGP (Fox to ESPN) proves the point. Before showing anything for 2027, the 2027 announcements (IMSA schedule usually mid-December, NASCAR network list in November) have to be checked.
- Outside the US the answer differs by country (Sky, Kayo, J SPORTS, ...): show the US card by default only when the phone's time zone is in the Americas, otherwise only "Where to watch near you" with the series' own page.
- Still to verify before building: GT World Australia TV (Speedcafe's Phillip Island broadcast article was not opened), whether FIAWEC+ sells in the US, the exact ESPN MotoGP coverage (ESPN, ESPN+ or Disney+), the IMSA/NBC contract end date, and the Hub's existing "Watch here" live stream (YouTube) so the two do not repeat each other.
