Trackside Pass

Privacy policy and terms. Last updated October 18, 2026. This is a plain-language draft for a personal project; it will be reviewed before the App Store release.

What Trackside Pass is

A race-day companion for motorsport fans: track maps and walking directions, schedules, weather, results, a passport of stamps and a free game (Pick your podium). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any racing series, team, driver, circuit or Apple. Series and track names are used only to identify them.

You can use it without an account

Everything works with no account. Your picks, stamps, starred sessions, saved spots and settings are kept on your own device. If you choose to sign in, some of it is also stored on our server (below).

What we store if you create an account

  • Your email address, used only to send you a sign-in code. It is held by our sign-in provider and is never shown to other players.
  • Your display name (a name you choose; do not use your real name if you prefer not to), whether you appear on the Everyone leaderboard, and your friend code.
  • Your picks for each race weekend, with the time they were made, the win chance and bonus that applied, and the points they earned.
  • Your passport (the tracks, race weekends and days you checked in at, and the badges you earned, with the times). It stays on your phone and, if you sign in, a copy is stored so others can open it.
  • Your friends list (who you added with a code, and who added you).
  • Reports you send about another player.

Other players can see your display name, your points and your passport. If your account is public (the default, a switch in Account) anyone can; if you switch it off only your friends can. Friends can also see when you have checked in at a track on a race weekend (the Friends here card) and, if you are friends, your picks once a race has started. They cannot see your email.

What stays on your device

Your location is used on your device to show where you are on the map and to give walking directions. It is not sent to us. Passport stamps, the races you are going to, alerts and settings are stored on your device.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts sign-in and the database for accounts.
  • Cloudflare hosts the app and its small servers (live results and the live stream lookup). Like any web host it sees technical request data such as your IP address.
  • Weather and radar: the app asks Open-Meteo and RainViewer for the forecast and radar at a track.
  • YouTube: when you press Watch here, the stream is played by YouTube and its terms and privacy policy apply to that video.

We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking for advertising.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Until you delete your account. In the app: Settings, Passport and your data, Delete my account. That removes your profile, picks, friendships and friend code from the server for good. Anything stored on your device stays there until you delete the app. To ask about your data you can also write to the address below.

The game

Pick your podium is a free game for fun. There are no prizes, no money and no betting. The win chances shown are our own statistical estimates from published results, not betting odds, predictions or advice. Scores come from the series' official results; if a series changes a classification, scores can change.

Fair play and conduct

Choose a name that is not offensive or misleading and do not impersonate anyone. We can remove names or accounts, hide players from leaderboards, or reset scores that break these rules or try to cheat the game. You can report a player from the leaderboard.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and the app will say so.
